# Commercial Licensing

Thank you for your interest in using software under the AW Non-Commercial License 3.0.0 for commercial purposes.

Commercial use is not permitted under the public license text without prior written permission.

## Contact

Please send inquiries to:

**commercial@ahliweb.com**

## Include the following information

When requesting commercial rights, please include:

1. Your name and organization
2. Project or product name
3. Intended use case
4. Whether use is internal or customer-facing
5. Whether the software will be sold, hosted, sublicensed, or used in paid services
6. Expected scale of use
7. Your preferred licensing timeline

## Typical commercial cases

Commercial permission is typically required for:

- SaaS or hosted platforms
- paid internal business operations
- client work or consulting
- paid support or managed services
- resale, sublicensing, or bundling in commercial products

## Contract preparation and evidence

Use the [non-binding commercial agreement framework](docs/commercial-license-agreement-framework.md) for the software release/commit, parties, expressly granted SaaS/white-label/internal/deployment rights, fees, duration, trademarks, derivative works, provenance, dispute resolution and optional Sharia-aligned ethical review.

Written permission requires actual agreement by authorized rightsholder(s); an inquiry, invoice, unauthenticated email, silence or download **alone** is not an authorization. Third-party dependencies and contributors remain subject to their own rights. Consult [evidence register](docs/evidence-and-references.md).

For applicants who voluntarily wish Sharia-aligned contracting, discuss clear subject matter, informed mutual consent, transparent consideration, fair remediation and existing valid third-party rights. No certification is claimed and non-Muslim applicants are not required to adopt religious beliefs.

## Versioned agreement and religious commitment

When negotiating a commercial grant, clearly identify the selected **AW-NC license version** (`3.0.0` if expressly adopted) and the Software release/commit. Refer to [Section 5 of the official license](LICENSE.md#5-contractual-covenant-amanah-and-sharia-aligned-commitment) for its mutual honesty undertaking and the licensor's solemn accountability before Allah Jalla wa 'Ala. No conversion, oath, religious affiliation or acceptance of a religious tribunal is required.

Provide both parties an opportunity to review complete contract terms and applicable law, and maintain an immutable signed copy. A separate commercial agreement may have different expressly agreed rights and obligations. Older grants do not automatically change because this repository updated its canonical text.


## Commercial use of mixed-license software

Any AW-NC commercial authorization covers only specific, expressly licensed AW-controlled copyrights. The agreement **does not supersede third-party licenses** or confer sublicensing over rights AW does not own. Provide [COMPONENT-LICENSE-MAP.md](COMPONENT-LICENSE-MAP.md) evidence showing all major components, their original licenses, and provenance, including GPL/AGPL/copyleft compatibility analysis.
