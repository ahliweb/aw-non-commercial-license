## Changes

New operative **Section 8** preserves third-party and pre-existing licenses according to their actual legal scope, while protecting new independently licensable original developments that authorized rightsholders expressly designate AW-NC. Neither automatic relicensing of all modifications nor GPL/AGPL override is allowed.

Added research comparison of seven licensing approaches, [LICENSE-MAP](../LICENSE-MAP.md) template, migration examples, evidence references, updated FAQ and SemVer-integrity checks. Existing Section 5 contractual Islamic amanah and Sections 6–7 civil-law/version safeguards are retained.
