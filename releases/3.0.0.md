# AW Non-Commercial License 3.0.0 — Release Record

**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **MAJOR legal license scope change** · [Canonical LICENSE](../LICENSE.md) · [VERSION](../VERSION).

## Changes

New operative **Section 8** preserves third-party and pre-existing licenses according to their actual legal scope, while protecting new independently licensable original developments that authorized rightsholders expressly designate AW-NC. Neither automatic relicensing of all modifications nor GPL/AGPL override is allowed.

Added research comparison of seven licensing approaches, [COMPONENT-LICENSE-MAP](../COMPONENT-LICENSE-MAP.md) template, migration examples, evidence references, updated FAQ and SemVer-integrity checks. Existing Section 5 contractual Islamic amanah and Sections 6–7 civil-law/version safeguards are retained.
