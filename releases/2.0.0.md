## Release scope

This release publishes the complete **AW-NC 2.0.0** licensing text. Section 5 makes a contractual undertaking to deal truthfully and in good faith by licensor and Licensee, and expressly acknowledges the licensor's Islamic solemn accountability before Allah Jalla wa 'Ala as Supreme Sovereign, Lawgiver and ultimate Judge of this world and the Hereafter. It contains no forced profession of religion, oath, religious-court requirement or faith-dependent civil rights. Sections 6–7 preserve national civil-law interpretation and non-retroactive version selection.
