# FAQ

## Is this license open source?

No. It is a custom source-available non-commercial license.

## Can I use software under this license for personal learning?

Yes, if the use is non-commercial.

## Can a university use it for teaching?

Generally yes, if the use is non-commercial.

## Can a company use it internally?

Not without permission if the use supports business operations, revenue generation, or commercial service delivery.

## Can a freelancer use it for a client?

No, not without commercial permission.

## Can I modify and redistribute it?

Yes, for non-commercial purposes and subject to the license terms.

## Can I sell software under this license?

No, unless you have prior written permission.

## Where do I request commercial permission?

From the authorized rightsholder of the specific software. For AhliWeb's own products/services that is **commercial@ahliweb.com**. For any other project, use the contact that project's rightsholder published; see [COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSING.md).

## I adopted AW-NC for my own project. What must I change? / Saya mengadopsi AW-NC untuk proyek saya sendiri, apa yang harus diubah?

Replace the Section 2 destination address in **your copy** (and every notice and `mailto:` link) with the email of your authorized rightsholder or representative. `commercial@ahliweb.com` is only for AhliWeb products/services within AhliWeb's authority. If you do not replace it, requests go to AhliWeb, **will not be answered** for non-AhliWeb projects, are not forwarded, carry no response-time commitment, and no answer is never permission. Ganti email tujuan Pasal 2 pada salinan Anda dengan email pemegang hak/wakil resmi; jika tidak, permohonan tetap ke AhliWeb, tidak ditanggapi untuk proyek non-AhliWeb, dan **diam bukan persetujuan**. Details: [adoption guide](docs/adoption-guide.md#customize-commercial-licensing-contact--penyesuaian-kontak-lisensi-komersial). This is non-binding guidance, not a commercial grant.

## Does AW-NC allow a paid SaaS or revenue-supporting internal CRM?

No without prior written commercial permission under AW-NC 1.0 section 1. Both cases are expressly within its definition of Commercial Purpose.

## Is AW-NC similar to Aladdin/AFPL?

Both are non-OSI source-available approaches, but the key distinction is that AFPL section 2(b) does not restrict functional running, while AW-NC expressly restricts covered commercial uses. AFPL imposes source/notice obligations on distributed derived works. See [comparison](docs/comparison.md).

## Is Islamic contract ethics a legally binding extra restriction?

No. [The guidance](docs/islamic-contractual-ethics.md) is optional ethical explanation, not an amendment to LICENSE.md, not a new license condition, and not an official Sharia certification.

## Does a community contribution become commercially re-licensable automatically?

No. Document the contributor's copyright and authorized grant, and separately check third-party licenses before granting commercial rights.

## How can I verify the scientific and juristic citations?

Review the [evidence register](docs/evidence-and-references.md), which distinguishes peer-reviewed research, primary law, institutional fatwa/resolution, standards, and license text.

## Which license edition is current?

The canonical [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) is AW-NC **3.0.0**, identified by [VERSION](VERSION). The historical [1.0.0](licenses/AW-NC-1.0.0.md) and [2.0.0](licenses/AW-NC-2.0.0.md) are archived unchanged. Each adopter project must explicitly designate its intended license version; central repository updates are not retroactive.

## Why a MAJOR version, not a PATCH?

The preceding 2.0.0 introduced Section 5's mutual contractual commitments and the licensor's solemn Islamic accountability declaration; Section 6 and Section 7 clarify civil law and version scope. The newly operative Section 8 also changes the scope clarification and therefore requires a MAJOR version. See [versioning policy](docs/semantic-versioning.md).

## Does Section 5 require anyone to be Muslim or swear an oath?

No. AW-NC 3.0.0 states the licensor's own solemn commitment before Allah Jalla wa 'Ala, and refers to voluntary moral accountability of Muslim Licensees. All users have the same conduct-based legal terms. No religious conversion, ritual, oath, special religious penalty or religious-court agreement is required.

## Does this create a separate enforceable contract directly with Allah?

No. The civil legal parties remain the licensor and licensee. The licensor's Islamic affirmation expresses moral and spiritual accountability **before** Allah according to Islamic belief. Civil enforceability and remedies remain subject to applicable law. Consult [Indonesian explanation](docs/akad-syari-id.md).

## Has AW-NC 3.0.0 been certified Sharia compliant or approved by a court?

No such certification or legal opinion is claimed. References to the Qur'an, IIFA and MUI substantiate a normative ethical foundation, **not** an official endorsement of this precise license text.

## Can I switch another author's contribution to 2.0.0?

Not without verifying the contribution's rights and relevant authorization; central repository publication does not transfer third-party copyright.


## Does AW-NC replace licenses present alongside it?

No. AW-NC §8 explicitly preserves other valid licenses (including MIT, Apache, GPL, AGPL and proprietary terms) **within the legal scope of their own protected material and any relevant derivative/combined work**.

## Are all new software modifications automatically AW-NC?

No. Original independently licensable enhancements are covered only when authorized rightsholders **expressly designate** them as AW-NC. New changes to existing GPL/AGPL code may continue to be governed by copyleft requirements.

## Can a paid AW license authorize a third-party SDK?

No. A grant by AW cannot automatically sublicense code or patents controlled by a different author. Verify those terms separately.

## Does placing GPL and AW modules in separate directories avoid GPL obligations?

Not necessarily. Assess actual copyright-based derivative/combined work status; an incompatible combined distribution cannot be made lawful by a folder separation or a new NOTICE file.

## Which other licenses resemble AW-NC?

See [the complete comparison](docs/comparison.md): Aladdin AFPL, PolyForm Noncommercial, Business Source License, Commons Clause, SSPL, Elastic License 2.0, with MIT as an open-source baseline.

## How are mixed licenses documented?

Use [COMPONENT-LICENSE-MAP.md](COMPONENT-LICENSE-MAP.md), file-level notices, and SPDX/SBOM. For the same copyright work, `OR` is a genuine rights-holder-granted choice; `AND` indicates legitimately cumulative conditions. Worked scenarios: [mixed-license fixtures](fixtures/mixed-license) and [SBOM guidance](docs/sbom-and-provenance.md) (expected decisions are working decisions accepted by the AhliWeb team, not independently reviewed).
