# FAQ

## Is this license open source?

No. It is a custom source-available non-commercial license.

## Can I use software under this license for personal learning?

Yes, if the use is non-commercial.

## Can a university use it for teaching?

Generally yes, if the use is non-commercial.

## Can a company use it internally?

Not without permission if the use supports business operations, revenue generation, or commercial service delivery.

## Can a freelancer use it for a client?

No, not without commercial permission.

## Can I modify and redistribute it?

Yes, for non-commercial purposes and subject to the license terms.

## Can I sell software under this license?

No, unless you have prior written permission.

## Where do I request commercial permission?

commercial@ahliweb.com

## Does AW-NC allow a paid SaaS or revenue-supporting internal CRM?

No without prior written commercial permission under AW-NC 1.0 section 1. Both cases are expressly within its definition of Commercial Purpose.

## Is AW-NC similar to Aladdin/AFPL?

Both are non-OSI source-available approaches, but the key distinction is that AFPL section 2(b) does not restrict functional running, while AW-NC expressly restricts covered commercial uses. AFPL imposes source/notice obligations on distributed derived works. See [comparison](docs/comparison.md).

## Is Islamic contract ethics a legally binding extra restriction?

No. [The guidance](docs/islamic-contractual-ethics.md) is optional ethical explanation, not an amendment to LICENSE.md, not a new license condition, and not an official Sharia certification.

## Does a community contribution become commercially re-licensable automatically?

No. Document the contributor's copyright and authorized grant, and separately check third-party licenses before granting commercial rights.

## How can I verify the scientific and juristic citations?

Review the [evidence register](docs/evidence-and-references.md), which distinguishes peer-reviewed research, primary law, institutional fatwa/resolution, standards, and license text.

## Which license edition is current?

The canonical [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) is AW-NC **2.0.0**, identified by [VERSION](VERSION). The original [1.0.0](licenses/AW-NC-1.0.0.md) is archived unchanged. Each adopter project must explicitly designate its intended license version; central repository updates are not retroactive.

## Why a MAJOR version, not a PATCH?

The operative Section 5 introduces mutual contractual commitments and the licensor's solemn Islamic accountability declaration; Section 6 and Section 7 clarify civil law and version scope. That is a substantive change, not merely a link or typographical correction. See [versioning policy](docs/semantic-versioning.md).

## Does Section 5 require anyone to be Muslim or swear an oath?

No. AW-NC 2.0.0 states the licensor's own solemn commitment before Allah Jalla wa 'Ala, and refers to voluntary moral accountability of Muslim Licensees. All users have the same conduct-based legal terms. No religious conversion, ritual, oath, special religious penalty or religious-court agreement is required.

## Does this create a separate enforceable contract directly with Allah?

No. The civil legal parties remain the licensor and licensee. The licensor's Islamic affirmation expresses moral and spiritual accountability **before** Allah according to Islamic belief. Civil enforceability and remedies remain subject to applicable law. Consult [Indonesian explanation](docs/akad-syari-id.md).

## Has AW-NC 2.0.0 been certified Sharia compliant or approved by a court?

No such certification or legal opinion is claimed. References to the Qur'an, IIFA and MUI substantiate a normative ethical foundation, **not** an official endorsement of this precise license text.

## Can I switch another author's contribution to 2.0.0?

Not without verifying the contribution's rights and relevant authorization; central repository publication does not transfer third-party copyright.
